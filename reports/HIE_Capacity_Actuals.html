HIE Capacity Actuals

The story

    Revenue HIE delivered late, by the month it was promised

    Value at selling price of units HIE committed (PI date) for each month, split by how late they actually finished. "Still open" = past its PI date and not yet produced. This is revenue pushed out, not necessarily lost — lost sales depend on whether Jacksonville had cover.

    Overdue backlog at HIE

    Value of units past their original PI date and not yet produced, at each weekly report.

    How late HIE finishes, per PO line

    Finish date vs original PI date, units. Finished lines only.

    HIE output per month

    Units through production (good + rejects), from week-over-week movement on the open order list. Months with missing weekly reports are spread evenly.

    Reject rate per month

    B-grade + defect units as % of units through production.

    What HIE committed on its PIs

    From HIE's own PI confirmations and monthly pending order lists in your mailbox. Each PO line's commitment is the PI date on HIE's first quote; later re-dates and re-prices are tracked as revisions.

    Price changes HIE made after quoting

    Same PO line, first quote vs latest quote.

    Cancellations, reductions and holds

    PO changes found in HIE threads. HIE's response and any charge it asked for are quoted from the email.

    HIE price changes year over year

    HIE's quoted unit price per SKU per PO (PI confirmations and pending order lists; warehouse inventory list where no quote was found). Year = price year (the PO's earliest PI date). Change is measured SKU by SKU on items bought in both years, weighted by units, so mix shifts don't distort it. $ impact = price change × units ordered that year.

    Price increases HIE announced

    HIE's own price tables in PI threads, showing the prior year's final price next to the new year's price.

    Price change by product line

    Unit-weighted % change vs prior year; $ = extra (or saved) cost on that year's units.

    Price history by SKU

    Unit-weighted HIE price by PO year. Sorted by cumulative $ impact of increases.

    Same SKU, different price in the same year

    SKUs where HIE charged more than 3% apart on different POs within one year — worth checking against the PI.

    By product line

    Sorted by late revenue. Click a header to sort.

    By SKU

    Every HIE item on the open order list since . Slip = days finished after original PI date (negative = early). Late $ = value of units finished more than days late, plus value overdue today.

    Open PO lines past their PI date today

    As of the latest HIE report. Latest PI = the PI date on HIE's most recent list (HIE sometimes moves it earlier as well as later); Re-sched = times it changed.
