HIE Capacity Actuals

The story

    Revenue HIE delivered late, by the month it was promised

    Value at selling price of units HIE committed (PI date) for each month, split by how late they actually finished. "Still open" = past its PI date and not yet produced. This is revenue pushed out, not necessarily lost — lost sales depend on whether Jacksonville had cover.

    Overdue backlog at HIE

    Value of units past their original PI date and not yet produced, at each weekly report.

    How late HIE finishes, per PO line

    Finish date vs original PI date, units. Finished lines only.

    HIE output per month

    Units through production (good + rejects), from week-over-week movement on the open order list. Months with missing weekly reports are spread evenly.

    Reject rate per month

    B-grade + defect units as % of units through production.

    Every HIE item on the open order list since . Slip = days finished after original PI date (negative = early). Late $ = value of units finished more than days late, plus value overdue today.

    Open PO lines past their PI date today

    As of the latest HIE report. Latest PI = the PI date on HIE's most recent list (HIE sometimes moves it earlier as well as later); Re-sched = times it changed.
